Privacy Policy — TowerDeChess

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

TowerDeChess — Chess Tower Defense ("TowerDeChess", "the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles.

1. No Personal Data Collected

TowerDeChess does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or analytics telemetry. The App does not require account creation or sign-in, and does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Game progress (unlocked campaign levels, star ratings, difficulty preference, and audio mute settings) is stored exclusively on your own device using local browser/WebView storage (localStorage). You can clear this data at any time by clearing app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

3. Children's Privacy

Because TowerDeChess does not collect any personal data from any user, it complies with children's privacy regulations including COPPA and GDPR-K.

4. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce online features (such as cloud saves or leaderboards), this privacy policy will be updated prior to release.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project repository at github.com/wildvegangoose/TowerDefense.
